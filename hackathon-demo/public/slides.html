INTENT PROTOCOL · ENCODE 2026

A safer way to pay for work.

Hire work without moving the goalposts. Freeze the rules before the work. Prove the outcome after.

On devnet, the pay is locked with the rule. A matching delivery is paid once. A wrong delivery is not paid. No token.

02 · FREEZE THE RULES

The criteria are written before delivery.

A cafe posts a writing bounty before the writer starts. The job text is saved with the bounty. The agreed headline is saved only as a check. After posting, neither side can edit what “done” means.

03 · ON-CHAIN ESCROW

The pay is locked when the bounty is posted.

  • A cafe locks 0.05 practice SOL with a writing bounty.
  • The writer turns in a headline. The program compares it with the headline locked at posting.
  • The agreed headline is paid once. A shorter headline leaves the pay locked.
  • The cafe can cancel before the check and take the pay back. Paying twice is rejected.

04 · LIVE DEMO

Three minutes, one devnet wallet.

  1. Open intent-protocol-wine.vercel.app. Install Phantom. Switch to Devnet, the free practice network.
  2. Press Get practice SOL, or use faucet.solana.com.
  3. Post the Northwind Coffee bounty. Submit Northwind Coffee: order ahead. Check and pay. The pay stays locked.
  4. Submit Northwind Coffee: order ahead, skip the line. Check and pay. Open Explorer. Pay again. It is refused.
