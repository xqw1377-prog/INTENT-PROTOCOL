INTENT PROTOCOL · ENCODE 2026

A safer way to pay for work.

Hire work without moving the goalposts. Freeze the rules before the work. Prove the outcome after.

On devnet, the pay is locked with the rule. A matching delivery is paid once. A wrong delivery is not paid. No token.

02 · FREEZE THE RULES

The criteria are written before delivery.

The creator stores the criteria text on the intent account. The program hashes that text in the same transaction. The expected submission is stored only as a hash. The answer itself is not on the account.

After that, the deliverer cannot edit the rule, and the creator cannot quietly change what “done” means.

03 · ON-CHAIN ESCROW

The SOL is locked when the intent is created.

  • A program-owned vault holds the reward. The sample intent locks 0.05 SOL.
  • Submit records the bytes and their hash. Verify recomputes sha256 and compares it to the frozen hash.
  • A match writes a verification-result hash and pays the deliverer once. A mismatch leaves the SOL locked.
  • Cancel before verification returns the escrow to the creator. A second settle is rejected.
